# Input Notes

- Main finding: students with structured peer feedback improved revision quality more than students with only teacher comments.
- Improvement was strongest in organization and coherence, weaker in grammar accuracy.
- Similar trend reported by Lee 2021 and Santos 2023.
- Our sample size was small: 48 students from one department.
- Possible reason: peer feedback made students re-read more actively and compare drafts more carefully.
- Cannot claim long-term writing improvement because intervention lasted only 6 weeks.
